FEATURED PROJECT

Iowa Customs

Driven to Be Bold

A marketing project focused on building brand awareness, creating engaging social media content, and showcasing aftermarket semi-truck parts and accessories.

View Project

01 — OVERVIEW

Project Overview

The Company

Iowa Customs is a semi-truck accessory manufacturing company based in Carroll, Iowa. The company provides aftermarket parts and accessories designed to help truck owners personalize and enhance their trucks.

The Goal

The goal of this project was to develop marketing content that communicates the Iowa Customs brand, highlights its products, and connects with its target audience.

02 — MY ROLE

What I Did

Social Media

Researched industry trends and developed content ideas designed to increase engagement and strengthen the company's social media presence.

Content Strategy

Used audience and industry insights to adjust posting strategies and create content that better connects with customers.

Sales Materials

Created product sales sheets featuring product options, sizing, finishes, and part numbers to support the sales process.
